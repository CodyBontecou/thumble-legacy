About Thumble

Phone gamepad controls for World of Warcraft on Mac, built by Cody Bontecou.

What Thumble does

Thumble turns your phone into a gamepad for World of Warcraft on your Mac. The Mac runs the game, ConsolePort manages in-game bindings, and the phone sends stick, button, shoulder and trigger input over the same Wi-Fi. Thumble Receiver is the Mac companion that delivers this input to the game.

Who it is for

Thumble is for WoW players on macOS 15 or later who want ConsolePort-style controls on their phone. Choose a ConsolePort release compatible with your exact WoW client. Thumble does not stream WoW to a phone. Windows and Linux receivers are not available.

Availability and pricing

The free signed and notarized Mac receiver is available now. The phone app is coming soon. The phone app offers a 30-minute total connected-use demo; the planned $19.99 USD one-time Pro unlock removes that time limit. No AI account is required for the built-in ConsolePort controller.
