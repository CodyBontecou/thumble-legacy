Thumble support

Need a hand? Email codybontecou@gmail.com. Include your phone model, OS version, Thumble version and a short description of the problem. Do not include passwords, authentication tokens or payment details.

Get connected

  1. Download Thumble Receiver for macOS 15 or later. Apple silicon and Intel are supported.
  2. Drag the receiver from the DMG into Applications and open it. Quit older receivers so only one is running.
  3. Connect your phone and Mac to the same local network and scan a fresh pairing code from the phone app. Allow local-network access when asked.

The new phone app is being prepared for release. Join launch updates or read the setup guide.

Demo and Pro

The demo includes every feature for 30 minutes total of connected controller use across sessions. Setup, local practice, menus and time spent paused or disconnected do not consume the allowance. Pro removes the time limit for a one-time $19.99 USD purchase in the phone app. Local prices may vary.

If you already purchased Pro, open Pro in the phone app and choose Restore Purchases with the same store account. Connect to the internet and pair your Mac so verified access can be delivered to the receiver. Apple and Android purchases are separate.

Apple handles iOS billing and refund requests through Report a Problem. For purchase trouble, tell us what the app displays; do not send your card details.

Input, layouts and dictation

If pairing works but input does not, check that the session is started, there is remaining demo time or verified Pro access, and the game accepts virtual gamepad input. Use local practice to check the controls before playing.

Custom layouts use your own connected agent. If a layout does not arrive, use the phone’s agent-connection and delivery checks. Optional Cloud Controllers requires its own sign-in and a compatible connected agent.

If dictation is unavailable, check microphone/speech permissions and language support, or type your message instead. Review the transcript before sending.
