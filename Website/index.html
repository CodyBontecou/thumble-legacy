A few things to know

Before you jump in.

Is the demo 30 minutes a day?

No. You get 30 minutes total of connected controller use, shared across sessions. The allowance does not refill daily. Setup, menus, local practice, and time spent paused or disconnected don’t consume demo time.

What do I get with Pro?

Unlimited controller time for a one-time $19.99 USD purchase in the phone app. The standard controller, custom layouts, saved profiles, and dictation are already included in the demo. Pro only removes the time limit.

What happens when the demo ends?

Live controller input and chat delivery stop when your allowance runs out. Your saved layouts remain available, and you can still set up and practice locally. Unlock Pro to continue connected use.

Do I need an AI agent?

The standard controller works without an agent. Custom layouts use your own connected agent through Thumble’s MCP tools. An AI subscription or hosted agent service is not included with Pro.

Can I use it with any game?

Thumble sends your button presses through the Mac receiver’s virtual gamepad. Support depends on the game and its controller settings. Each gameplay press comes from you; Thumble does not play the game for you.

Can I install the phone app now?

The new phone app is coming soon. Download the Mac receiver now and join the launch list for release news. Public store download links will be added when available.
