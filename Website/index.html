A few things to know

Before you jump in.

Is the demo 30 minutes a day?

No. You get 30 minutes total of connected controller use, shared across sessions. The allowance does not refill daily. Setup, menus, local practice, and time spent paused or disconnected don’t consume demo time.

What do I get with Pro?

Unlimited controller time for a one-time $19.99 USD purchase in the phone app. ConsolePort controls, stick calibration, and dictation are included in the demo. Pro only removes the time limit.

What happens when the demo ends?

Live controller input and chat delivery stop when your allowance runs out. Your saved layouts remain available, and you can still set up and practice locally. Unlock Pro to continue connected use.

What do I need to play?

A Mac with macOS 15 or later, WoW, the matching ConsolePort addon, the Thumble phone app, and both devices on the same Wi-Fi. No AI agent or cloud account is needed for the ConsolePort controller. The phone app is coming soon.

What is the Thumble addon?

It is our recommended WoW companion addon for Mac gamepad recovery after a receiver restart. ConsolePort handles the bindings. The first Classic Era release is awaiting CurseForge approval. Read the CurseForge installation steps.

Which WoW versions work?

Our initial focus is WoW on Mac with ConsolePort. Use a ConsolePort release that supports the exact WoW client you launch; compatibility depends on that client and addon version. The setup guide walks you through choosing the installation and checking it in-game.

Does WoW run on my phone?

No. WoW runs on your Mac, and your phone is the controller. Keep the Mac and phone on the same Wi-Fi. Thumble does not stream the game to your phone.

Can I install the phone app now?

The new phone app is coming soon. Download the Mac receiver now and join the launch list for release news. Public store download links will be added when available.
