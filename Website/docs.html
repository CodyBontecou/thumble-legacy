Your phone and your Mac.

The new Thumble app turns your phone into a controller for your Mac. A small local receiver creates a virtual gamepad and receives your inputs over your local network. Start with the standard controller or use your own connected agent to generate a custom layout.

The Mac receiver is available now. The phone app is coming soon; join the launch list for release updates. The receiver requires the phone app for controller use.

What you need

  • A Mac running macOS 15 or later, with Apple silicon or an Intel processor.
  • The new Thumble phone app when released. Android support is in development; Windows and Linux receivers are not available yet.
  • Both devices connected to the same local network.
  • A game that accepts the receiver’s virtual gamepad input. Controller support and bindings vary by game.

Install and pair

  1. Download Thumble Receiver for Mac.
  2. Open the DMG and drag Thumble Receiver into Applications.
  3. Open the receiver from Applications. The app is signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple.
  4. Once you have the new phone app, open it with both devices on the same local network. Scan the receiver’s pairing code from the phone, or use the pairing link.
  5. Follow the phone’s setup steps, choose your controller, then explicitly start the connected demo when you are ready.

Pairing codes expire. If a code no longer works, use the receiver’s current code. Run one receiver at a time; quit an older Thumble companion before using this receiver.

Release 1.0.0 is universal for Apple silicon and Intel. Download its SHA-256 checksum.

The standard controller

Use familiar buttons, sticks, and triggers without an AI agent. Calibrate the controller and practice locally before sending input. Connected controller use begins when you start the session; pause or disconnect when you take a break.

The receiver appears in the Mac menu bar. Keep it running while you use your phone as a controller.

Layouts made with your agent

Connect your own AI agent to Thumble’s MCP tools. Describe the controls and arrangement you want, have your agent validate and save the profile, then activate it on your phone. Use the phone’s setup checks to confirm the agent connection and layout delivery.

Saved profiles and layout switching are included in the demo. The standard controller works without an agent. Your own agent service or subscription is separate from Thumble Pro.

The new app uses agent-generated layouts rather than the older Mac visual editor. Agent integration setup will accompany the phone release.

Dictation and game chat

Dictate a message or type it, review and edit the text, and send it using the chat controls. Dictation depends on your device, supported language, and microphone permissions. Game chat delivery depends on your game’s setup.

Dictation and transcript editing are included in the demo. Sending chat to the game requires remaining connected-use time or Pro.

30-minute demo and Pro

The free demo includes 30 minutes total of connected controller use, shared across sessions. It is a one-time allowance and does not refill daily. The standard controller, calibration, agent-generated layouts, saved profiles, and dictation are all included.

Setup, menus, local practice, and time spent paused or disconnected do not consume your allowance. Active connected sessions count, including idle time and chat composition.

When the allowance ends, live input and chat delivery stop. Your layouts remain available and local setup and practice still work.

Thumble Pro is a one-time $19.99 USD in-app purchase that removes the connected-use time limit. There is no subscription and no separate feature unlock. Local store prices may vary. The phone app and Pro purchase are coming soon.

Use Restore Purchases in the phone app to restore an existing purchase with the same store account. Internet is needed to verify the purchase and restore it on a new receiver.

Game support

The Mac receiver sends virtual gamepad input. Check your game’s controller settings and bindings before playing. Custom layouts reflect your configuration; they do not imply automatic game recognition or universal compatibility.

Every gameplay press comes from you. Thumble does not read game state or automate gameplay. Historical blog posts describe earlier development work and may show the retired app and editor.

Other platforms

The signed download on this site is the Mac receiver. Android phone support is in development. Windows and Linux receivers are not currently offered as downloads.

If pairing or input does not work

  • Check that the receiver is open and both devices are on the same local network. Guest networks may isolate devices.
  • Allow local-network access when the phone asks, then scan a fresh pairing code.
  • Quit older receiver apps so only one Thumble receiver is running.
  • Confirm your game accepts controller input and check its bindings.
  • Check remaining demo time or restore your Pro purchase if live input is unavailable.

Pausing and disconnecting

Pausing or disconnecting stops live input and preserves remaining demo time. The receiver releases held controls when the session disconnects, the Mac sleeps, or the demo expires. Demo expiry can interrupt a session; check the remaining time before you start.
